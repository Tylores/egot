DELETE      | Single   | 200 (OK)    | Yes      | Content-Type

TRANSFORMATION PATTERNS:
---
Pattern 1: GET/DELETE with Body
  - Add: w.WriteHeader(http.StatusOK) before encoding
  - Change: Encode(nil) → Encode(&sep.ElementType{})
  - Keep: w.Header().Set("Content-Type", ...)

Pattern 2: POST to List
  - Add: w.Header().Set("location", "/service/{id}") first
  - Add: w.WriteHeader(http.StatusCreated) [201]
  - Change: Encode(nil) → Encode(&sep.ElementType{})
  - Keep: w.Header().Set("Content-Type", ...)

Pattern 3: HEAD
  - Remove: All headers and body encoding
  - Add: w.WriteHeader(http.StatusOK)
  - That's it!

Pattern 4: PUT/DELETE to List (Read-Only)
  - Remove: All headers and body encoding
  - Add: w.WriteHeader(http.StatusMethodNotAllowed) [405]
  - That's it!

HEADER ORDER (CRITICAL):
---

2. w.Header().Set("Content-Type", ...) [if body]
